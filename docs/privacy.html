Height Reply Bot: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Height Reply Bot is a private tool used only by the Instagram account @mav.tallteen to reply to comments on its own posts.

What it reads

It reads comments left on @mav.tallteen's own posts (the comment text and the commenter's username) through the official Instagram API, only to decide which emoji reply to post.

What it stores

Nothing. Comments are processed in memory and are not saved, sold, or shared with anyone.

Data deletion

Because no data is stored, there is nothing to delete. To stop the tool from interacting with your comments, delete your comment or ask @mav.tallteen in a direct message on Instagram.

Contact

Send a direct message to @mav.tallteen on Instagram.
